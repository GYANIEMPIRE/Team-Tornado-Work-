About us

About Team Tornado Works

Team Tornado is a support team for people who have lost access to an account or group, or who need harmful content taken down.

We work across Telegram, Instagram and Facebook. For each request we look at the platform's own rules, prepare the case properly and follow it through with you until it is resolved or we know why it cannot be.

Our aim is simple. You should always know what is happening with your request and what comes next.

What we handle

  • Account removal, ban and recovery
  • Channel and group removal, ban and recovery on Telegram
  • Story and post removal on Instagram
  • Account ban and account recovery on Facebook

Clear guidance

Your details stay private

We only ask for what the request needs. We never ask for passwords or one-time codes.

Honest expectations

Every platform makes its own final decision. We will not promise a result we cannot control.

Common questions

What should I send to start?

The username or link of the account, group or post, what happened, and any screenshots that help. Do not send passwords, OTPs or verification codes.

How long does a request take?

It depends on the platform and the case. We share an estimate once we have looked at your request.

Do you guarantee recovery or removal?

No. Telegram, Instagram and Facebook make the final decision. We prepare the request carefully and follow up with you.

Can I ask for someone else's account to be removed?

We help with your own accounts, groups and content, and with content that targets you. We do not take requests meant to harass or silence other people.

Where do I get updates?

Join our Telegram channel and the broadcast link at the bottom of any page.
